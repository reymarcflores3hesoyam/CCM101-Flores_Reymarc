# Mission Reflection

This mission helped me understand cloud computing better. Before this activity, I mostly thought of cloud computing as online storage and virtual servers. After exploring AWS, Azure, and GCP, I learned that they offer many different services. I was most impressed by **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** because of its Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data processing, and Kubernetes services. I also learned that AWS, Azure, and GCP have similar services, such as virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, and identity management.

I learned that choosing a cloud platform depends on the needs of a business or organization. Factors such as budget, security, performance, scalability, and existing technologies are important when making a decision. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft products may choose Azure because it works well with Microsoft technologies. On the other hand, an AI research company may choose GCP because of its AI and Machine Learning services. AWS can also be a good choice for organizations that need many different cloud services.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that it is important to understand the client's needs before choosing a platform. I learned that no single cloud platform is best for everyone. This mission also improved my GitHub portfolio because I added research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, cloud service comparisons, client recommendations, a decision matrix, and my KillerCoda Linux investigation. Overall, this activity improved my understanding of cloud computing and helped me organize my work in a clearer and more professional way.
