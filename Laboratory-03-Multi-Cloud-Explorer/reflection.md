# Mission Reflection

## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is Google Cloud Platform (GCP). I found its Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data processing, and Kubernetes services interesting. Before this activity, I mainly knew cloud computing as online storage and servers, but I learned that cloud platforms offer many different tools for developing and managing applications.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similar services, including virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, and identity management. They also have global infrastructure that allows organizations to run applications in different locations. Although their services have different names and features, they all provide scalable resources that can be selected based on an organization's needs.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements have a major influence on choosing a cloud platform because every organization has different goals and resources. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft products may find Azure more suitable, while an AI research company may benefit from GCP's AI and Machine Learning capabilities. Budget, security, performance, scalability, and existing technologies should all be considered before making a decision.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

I learned that being a Cloud Solutions Consultant requires more than just knowing the names of cloud services. It is important to understand the client's situation and determine which platform and services can best solve their problems. This activity also helped me practice comparing AWS, Azure, and GCP and explaining my recommendations based on business requirements.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved because I added research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, along with service comparisons and client recommendations.
